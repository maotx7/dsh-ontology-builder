# Service catalogue (fictional)

The Payments team owns the billing service and the invoice service.
The billing service is tier 1 and depends on the ledger service.
The ledger service is owned by the Core Finance team and is tier 1.
The invoice service is tier 2 and depends on the billing service.

The Growth team owns the referral service, which is tier 3.
The referral service depends on the billing service for payouts.
